# Policies

- **Sickness:** Phone the People team before 08:30 on the day. Inside the building, use Rosa's extension in whos-who.md. From outside, call 01632 960140. Message your lead as well. If you become unwell at work, tell your lead before you leave. Tell Tom if Anjali is out, or reception if both are out.
- **Expenses:** You need a receipt for anything over £25. See who-owns-what.md for who gives you the form and how-the-desk-works.md for approval.
- **Supplier gifts and hospitality:** Do not accept anything worth more than £20. Tell Aisha about every offer, including any you turn down. In week one, do not reply to the supplier yourself. Tell your lead, who replies. See who-owns-what.md for the gift register and cover.
- **Confidentiality:** Keep prices and deal sheets in the shared drive. Never forward them to personal email or paste them into a tool the company has yet to approve.
- **Working from home:** No home working in week one. After that, you can work from home up to two days a week by agreement with Anjali.

If a policy is not on this page, check the other reference files, then treat it as not in the folder.
