# Map of the place

The head office is Staithe House, 3 Canal Row, Calderwick. Calderwick is a made-up town. Use the green door on Canal Row. Reception is straight ahead.

| Floor | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| Ground | Reception, IT Service Desk (room G.03), post room, bike store, lift and an accessible toilet by reception. |
| 1 | Finance, People team, Legal, meeting rooms Lock and Towpath, and a kitchen. |
| 2 | IT and Purchasing, kitchen and quiet room Heron. The new buyer's desk is 2.14, by the window next to Anjali. |

There are kitchens on floors 1 and 2, none on the ground floor. Each has a kettle, fridge and microwave. Tea and coffee are free. Toilets are by the stairs on every floor.

The doors open by badge only, from 07:30 to 18:30. Your badge also opens the bike store.

The car park has 12 spaces and is permit only. See who-owns-what.md for who issues permits.

There is a first-aid box at reception and in each kitchen. Denise Hartley, the Office Manager, is the first aider and the fire warden. Find her at reception or call ext 100.

If there is a fire, leave by the stairs. Never use the lift. Go to the canal-side car park by the blue gate. In an emergency, call 999 first, then tell reception on ext 100.

This page repeats the safety names and numbers on purpose, so you can use it on its own.
