## Cover rules

- **Desk cover:** Tom covers Anjali's absences. Anjali covers Tom when she is in. If both are out, leave the decision in your notes tray, send both a Chat message and carry on with your queue. It waits. Nothing on the desk needs you to decide alone.
- **Reception cover:** Use Denise's extension in whos-who.md for the reception cover desk. The line is answered 08:00 to 17:30 on working days. Use it for building or safety problems. In an emergency, call 999 first, then tell reception. Routine requests wait when reception is closed.
- **IT cover:** The Service Desk answers Callum's extension in whos-who.md from 08:00 to 18:00 on working days. You can also raise a Mendbook ticket.
- **Contract cover:** If a contract question cannot wait for Jonathan, Anjali decides what to do. Tom makes that decision when she is out. The lead contacts the outside law firm if needed. A new buyer never contacts the firm. If both leads are out, use the desk cover rule.
- **From outside:** Call the switchboard on 01632 960142 and ask for the person or their extension. If you are running late, ask for reception.
- **Anything else:** Anjali is your first stop for anything this table does not list, even when she does not own it. Tell her what you need to find out. Tom covers when she is out. If both are out, use the desk cover rule.
