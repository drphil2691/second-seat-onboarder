# How the desk works

You are the second person on the IT Purchasing Desk. Until now, the lead has run the desk alone with an AI helper and a shared drive. The desk buys laptops, phones, clinic software, printers and network services for the clinics.

## The two seats

The lead prepares each deal. The buyer runs it with the supplier, within the limits the lead writes down. The week-one limits below apply first.

A **deal sheet** is one page for an approved deal, kept at `deals/<supplier>/deal-sheet.md`. It has four parts in order:

1. **Opening ask:** what we ask for first.
2. **Walk-away limit:** the most the desk will agree to pay.
3. **Red line:** a term we never agree to.
4. **Next action:** what happens next.

Keep the walk-away limit and red line internal. Never show or say either to a supplier.

Your **queue** is your list of tasks in order. Each person has a file at `queue/queue-<first-name>.md`. Your day-one queue is already written. See your-queue.md.

Your **notes tray** is where you write and save your desk work in week one. Use `notes-tray/<first-name>/` for call notes, questions and your end-of-day note. Use Mendbook and Chat for support and messages. Leave all other shared drive files alone in week one.

A **hand-back note** goes to the lead when a supplier asks for something the deal sheet does not cover. Leave the question unanswered and say, “I will come back to you on that”. Write what the supplier asked, what the sheet says and what is missing. Save it in your notes tray and tell the lead. See who-owns-what.md for the owner and cover. In week one, keep to the limits below and hand the question back internally.

## Approvals

- The new buyer approves nothing in week one.
- Anjali approves IT spend up to and including £10,000. Tom signs off anything over £10,000.
- After spend approval, Aisha checks the budget code and releases the purchase order in Tallyroll.
- Your lead approves expenses. Use who-owns-what.md for cover and policies.md for the receipt rule.

## Your limits in week one

Week one means your first five working days. No emails or calls to suppliers on your own. No purchase orders or signatures. Never forward anything outside the company. You sit in on calls and take notes. You do not speak for the company yet.
