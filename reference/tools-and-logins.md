# Tools and logins

Collect your laptop from Callum in person, in room G.03 on the ground floor. He gives you your username and sets up your authenticator with you. If you prefer to keep your own phone out of sign-in, ask Callum for a key fob.

## Sign in on day one

1. Open **Latchkey**, the company sign-in. Use one username for all your tools, with the authenticator app on your phone or the key fob.
2. Open **Gmail** and **Chat**. They are part of **Google Workspace**, along with Calendar, Meet and Drive.
3. Open the **Purchasing Desk** shared drive in Drive. It holds the desk's work. See how-the-desk-works.md for its layout.
4. Open your queue file. See how-the-desk-works.md for where to find it.

## Other tools

- **Tallyroll** is the finance system for raising purchase orders. Your access is read-only in week one.
- **Mendbook** is the IT help portal for tickets.
- **Burrow** is the company password manager. Callum shows you how to use it. This folder never holds passwords.

The laptop browser has a bookmark folder called “C&H Tools”. The same list is in `00-start-here/links.md` on the shared drive.

## If you get stuck

- **Locked out:** Stop trying after two attempts. Call the Service Desk on ext 200 or walk to Callum's room, G.03. Use who-owns-what.md for cover.
- **Shared drive will not open:** Raise a Mendbook ticket under “Access” or call the Service Desk. Anjali approves access and Callum switches it on. For contact details and cover, use whos-who.md and who-owns-what.md.
- **Wi-Fi:** The staff network is `CH-Staff`. Callum gives you a staff Wi-Fi card with your laptop. The card has the details you need to join. This folder does not hold them. If you lose the card, ask the Service Desk. Never ask a colleague to tell you the password.
