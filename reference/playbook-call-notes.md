# How to take call notes

Before the call, open the deal sheet. During the call, write down what you hear. Mark a point you missed so you can check it with the lead after the call.

Save the call notes as `notes-tray/<first-name>/YYYY-MM-DD-<supplier>-call.md`. Use the date of the call. Put any question that needs a decision in a hand-back note. See how-the-desk-works.md.

Use these six headings. The lines below them show a short practice example for the Fernhill call.

## Who was on the call

Anjali, the new buyer and the Fernhill representative.

## What the supplier said, in their words

“We'll check the figures and come back to you.”

## Numbers mentioned

No figures in this part of the call.

## What we said

Anjali asked the supplier to confirm the figures in writing.

## What we promised and by when

We made no promises.

## Anything outside the deal sheet

Nothing in this example.
